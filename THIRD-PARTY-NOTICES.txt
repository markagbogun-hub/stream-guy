Piper (text-to-speech) - NOT bundled. The optional Piper presenter voice runs a separate
  Piper install that you provide and point RadioCastOS to (AI Presenter Studio > PIPER SETUP).
  Piper's current engine is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3 (GPLv3); it is
  never included in or linked into RadioCastOS. If you choose DOWNLOAD in PIPER SETUP, the
  official release from https://github.com/rhasspy/piper and a voice from

Kokoro (text-to-speech) and sherpa-onnx - NOT bundled. The optional Kokoro presenter voice runs a
  separate sherpa-onnx program (sherpa-onnx-offline-tts.exe) with a Kokoro model that you provide
  and point RadioCastOS to (AI Presenter Studio > KOKORO SETUP). sherpa-onnx is by the k2-fsa
  project (Apache-2.0, https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx); the Kokoro model is from
  https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0). The sherpa-onnx speech data folder
  includes espeak-ng (GPLv3), which stays a separate program on your PC and is never linked into
  RadioCastOS. If you choose DOWNLOAD in KOKORO SETUP, the official sherpa-onnx release and the
  Kokoro model from https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases are fetched to your own user
  folder. Check the licence files that come with each download.
