# RadioCastOS 1.79.0

Adds a Pads & Timers page: twelve one-press jingle pads that play over the music, four countdown timers (a length or a time of day, with red overtime), and TRACK LEFT / TOP OF HOUR read-outs. The library gets an Add Files button and now accepts wma, opus, oga, aif, aiff and mp2. See docs/PADS_AND_TIMERS.md.
